HOW IT LEARNS

Snapshots become a price curve.

  1. 01
    Record each observation

    Repeated IDs update the item while preserving current bid, bid count, and time.

  2. 02
    Capture the final price

    Mark the listing ended and add the actual close to measure forecast error.

  3. 03
    Calibrate by category

    Median prediction ratios reveal whether a category tends to close above or below estimates.

Local results are descriptive, not a guarantee of future profit. Larger, cleaner samples make calibration more useful.
