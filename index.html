REAL PRICE EVIDENCE

Four price tiers, one conservative ceiling.

Each tier stays labeled so a retail asking price can never masquerade as a completed sale or cash offer.

01 · STRONGESTCompleted sold comps

Exact-model P20, median, average, P80, sell-through, and days to sell from an authorized feed.

02 · SPECIALTYPrice guide + yearly demand

Current collectible value, dealer buy/sell references, and reported annual units. Dealer values are not called pawn quotes.

03 · USED MARKETeBay + Google Shopping

Closely matched used offers combined across channels, then discounted before planning.

04 · FALLBACKNew-retail replacement

A multi-merchant new-price reference with a larger condition and resale haircut.

Decision rule: pawn requires precious-metal liquidation evidence. Retail requires a price plus sell-through, sufficient recent completed sales, or reported annual unit volume. Product reviews and active listings cannot pass demand by themselves.
